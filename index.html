03 / MATERIAL QUESTIONS

A proposal is clearer when its unknowns stay visible.

Four material decisions shape the direction. Each is intentionally left open for evidence, testing and a future maker to answer.

  1. 01

    Gold / alloy undecided

    proposed / unknown

    A warm metal treatment is proposed for the study; alloy, finish and source have not been decided.

  2. 02

    Crystal / geometry in study

    proposed / unresolved

    A clear crystal edge is drawn as a geometry study only; its material, dimensions and optical behaviour are unknown.

  3. 03

    Leather / source unresolved

    proposed / unknown

    A leather strap is proposed for the object; tannery, source, colour and replaceability need evidence before they can be named.

  4. 04

    Movement / architecture only

    illustrative / no calibre

    The movement is a drawn arrangement of parts. It makes no claim about a working calibre, performance, manufacture or service history.

04 / GOLD AS A SURFACE

Not shine.
Evidence of contact.

The proposed metal should show where light meets a hand: a restrained brush, a narrow highlight, and room for patina to become part of the reading.

Study note / brushing and patina are intended design questions, not material or ageing claims.

05 / MECHANISM AS ARCHITECTURE

Heirloom intent would begin with access.

These are hypothetical service principles for a future object. They are not promises of repairability, performance or longevity.

  1. 01

    Legible assembly

    A future movement could make its layers understandable to the person who opens it. That is a design intention, not evidence of a mechanism.

    hypothetical / to be engineered
  2. 02

    Repair access

    Access points could be considered from the first drawing, leaving room for a service model that has not yet been defined.

    proposed / service model unknown
  3. 03

    Replaceable strap

    A replaceable leather element would let wear remain visible without fixing one supplier, source or lifespan in advance.

    proposed / source unresolved
  4. 04

    Documented components

    Part names, drawings and changes could travel with a future object. No catalogue, record or certification exists for this study.

    intention only / evidence absent
Leather field / source unresolved

06 / LEATHER + TIME

Can wear become part of the object?

Patina is a question of care, not a guarantee of age.

The strap is imagined as a replaceable layer: something that could record use, leave the case intact, and be chosen again. Its source, treatment and construction remain open.

Source status / no tannery, supplier, provenance or production method is established.
